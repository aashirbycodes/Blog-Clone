PTA Gives Conditional Approval for Ufone-Telenor Rebranding


By Jehangir Nasir | Published Sep 4, 2026 | 10:50 pm





The rebranding of the merged Ufone-Telenor mobile operation is expected soon following regulatory approval, sources told ProPakistani. However, the date for the formal launch has not been confirmed.
Sources at the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) said the regulator had granted conditional approval for the rebranding, subject to completion of all required legal formalities.
They added that the authority was unaware of any plan to hold the rebranding event on September 15 and had not received confirmation of the date.
The Ufone-Telenor amalgamation has been completed under Pak Telecom Mobile Limited (PTML), bringing their spectrum resources, network infrastructure and customer base under a single mobile operation. The combined business serves more than 74 million subscribers across Pakistan.
Following the merger, management proposed replacing the existing Ufone and Telenor Pakistan identities with the global “e&” brand. PTA’s approval reportedly requires PTML to notify the regulator after completing the necessary legal requirements and before launching any commercial or promotional campaign under the new identity.
The proposed rebranding had previously been delayed amid legal and corporate governance questions surrounding the approval process.
While “e&” remains the proposed identity for the merged operation, PTML has yet to publicly announce the final brand name, launch date, or transition plan for Ufone and Telenor customers.
